# Lecture 12: Lighting Gallery

Unreal Engine **5.8.4**. Open `Lecture12.uproject`, then Play `/Game/Lecture12/Maps/L_LightingGallery` (the default map).

WASD / mouse / Space move, look and jump. Tab opens/resumes the pause menu. Its buttons resume, save, load, restart the run or quit. F5 saves, F9 loads, L switches day/evening and R restarts the run. Escape also opens/resumes the menu, but Unreal normally reserves it to stop PIE.

Collect the three gold shards and compare point, spot and rectangular lights, sun shadows, Lumen bounce and reflections. Saving restores the player, moving actor, shards and lighting. Restarting preserves the saved slot.

# Lecture 9 animation workshop

Open `Lecture9/Lecture9.uproject` in Unreal Engine 5.8.4. Start with `L_AnimationBasics`, then open `L_AnimationWorkshop` for the advanced examples.

Move with WASD, hold Left Shift to sprint, hold Left Ctrl to crouch and press F for the full-body montage. In the advanced workshop, use E for the upper-body montage and C for Sequencer. Stop and restart Play to reset.
